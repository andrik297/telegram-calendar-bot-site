Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Telegram Calendar Bot is a personal productivity application that allows an authorized user to create and manage Google Calendar events through Telegram.

Information We Access

The application may access Google Calendar data necessary to create, view, update, and delete calendar events at the user's request.

How Information Is Used

Google Calendar data is used solely to provide the calendar functionality requested by the user.

Data Sharing

Google Calendar data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials required to connect to Google Calendar may be securely stored by the application hosting environment.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security settings.
